# Project brief: vocab pathfinder

## The competition

- Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), national K–12 competition.
- Age group: Middle school (grades 6–8).
- Judges score a written entry (PDF up to 10 pages) and a video (up to 4 minutes).
- Registration deadline: December 18, 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern. Submission deadline: December 23, 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern (2:00 pm Pacific).

## The problem

Some students struggle with reading books and vocabulary because they miss or do not understand parts of class. The impact area is school and learning. The goal is a calm website where students can learn vocabulary, practice reading out loud, and receive a personal learning path.

Evidence and interviews in the original brief include lower average reading scores for many fourth-grade students in 2024, plus students who described falling behind when they could not focus or when class moved too quickly.

## My solution

Vocab Pathfinder helps students learn at their own pace, estimates their English understanding, and gives practice to improve. It should have no ads, energy limits, paid restrictions, streaks, ranks, currency, or leaderboard. It should feel personal and encouraging.

## Safety plan

The AI must be kind, never insulting or discouraging. It must not provide dangerous instructions, bullying messages, or inappropriate content. It should redirect harmful or unrelated requests to safe learning activities and encourage a trusted adult or emergency services for emergencies. It must not ask for full names, addresses, passwords, or school names. Progress is stored locally for now. Reading levels are estimates, not permanent labels. Students can report mistakes in a future version.

## My AI’s instructions

The student first takes a 20-question check covering English skills. When the level narrows down, the student takes a shorter check in that range to estimate a more exact starting point. The AI creates practice problems, tests, and activities to improve English. It uses vocabulary, sentence structure, reading, writing, and spelling. Problems and answers should be quick, clear, suitable for the student, and specific rather than vague.

## Version 1 boundary

Version 1 focuses on one complete journey: assessment, a personalized next step, and a short activity with the AI guide. It also includes small sample vocabulary and reading practice plus local progress tracking so the main job can be shown clearly in a short video.
